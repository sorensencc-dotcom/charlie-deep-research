## 3. Admission Criteria

**ADMITTED (independent primary/secondary):**
- Contemporary (1943-44) documentary record — not authored by Sorensen — independently corroborating the October 1943 functional sidelining date, OR
- Independent correspondence or oral history (Miller, Bricker, HFII, Bennett, or Ford executives) corroborating Bennett's role and the causal sequence, in terms consistent with the memoir, OR
- Two or more contemporary press accounts (Free Press + at least one other outlet) independently reporting the same causal account (not just the same wire-service text).

**CONTESTED-TAG only (insufficient for admission):**
- Bennett's memoir alone (interested witness on the opposing side — useful for triangulation, not sufficient alone for admission).
- Press coverage that reports the resignation as fact but explicitly declines to state a cause ("has not been officially disclosed") — confirms the event, not the mechanism.
- Later secondary histories repeating Sorensen's or Bennett's account without new primary sourcing.

**Remains BLOCKED:**
- No corroboration of the causal mechanism found beyond Sorensen's own account. Retain as memoir-attributed / open-question per R2, do not assert Bennett's specific role as settled fact.

## 4. Status Transition Rules

- BLOCKED → CONTESTED: Bennett memoir or press-confirms-event-not-cause found; scene retained with explicit dual-interested-witness framing, causal mechanism flagged as disputed.
- BLOCKED → ADMITTED: independent corroboration (non-Sorensen, non-Bennett) of both the timeline AND the causal mechanism found; scene may drop the single-source caveat.
- V-6.4 and V-6.6 remain gated on this packet's outcome — do not resolve those independently of this timeline finding without re-checking cross-link consistency.
- No qualifying source found after full sweep: remains BLOCKED, escalate to Tier 1 — decide whether to present as "contested, per family/Bennett accounts" or narrow the on-screen claim to only the undisputed facts (resignation date, location pattern).

## Known ambiguity: which confrontation is p.912?

Treatment v13 cites "acc. 65, box 69, p.912" for Sorensen's account of Bennett physically
threatening Edsel over a rumored kidnap plot against Edsel's sons — an incident tied to a
meeting with Robert Patterson, dated before Edsel's death in May 1943. That is almost
certainly a DIFFERENT scene from the 1944 exit story. Box 69 covers "Diary Notes; 1940-1944"
as one continuous manuscript — p.912 could sit anywhere in that span.

**Do not assume p.912 is about the 1944 removal until you've actually turned to that page.**
If it isn't, keep hunting the same box for whatever entry does cover Oct 1943 - Mar 1944.

## What changed and why

An earlier draft of this checklist proposed a specific Jan-March 1944 four-block chronology
(rest leave → confrontation → Florida/codicils → termination) with exact date ranges. None
of those dates are sourced — they were a plausible-sounding story built before any primary
document was in hand. That draft also assigned the Treatment's cited p.912 passage to a
January 1944 confrontation, when context suggests it's more likely the earlier (pre-May 1943)
Bennett/kidnap-threat scene. And it proposed diagnosing emotional state from handwriting
pressure, which isn't a real technique. This version replaces invented dates with the four
anchors that actually have a source, and replaces the pre-scripted four-act structure with an
open comparison log — consistent with the sourcing packets' own rule: log what's found, don't
assert the narrative in advance.

# CAST IRON CHARLIE — Treatment Draft Skeleton v1

**Governed by:** `TREATMENT_FRAMEWORK_SPEC_v1.md` (LOCKED). Any structural conflict resolves in the Spec's favor.
**Purpose:** Structural skeleton for the treatment. Defines what each section *does*, beat by beat, with transitions and rhythm. Not the treatment — no scenes, no dialogue, no prose paragraphs.
**Expansion target:** Codex expands each beat into treatment prose without adding, removing, or reordering beats.
**Master metaphor:** *The pattern outlives the patternmaker.* Every section must touch it at least once; touchpoints are marked ◆.

**Beat tags:** [EMO] emotional spine · [IND] industrial through-line · [GEO] geopolitical layer · [FRAME] framing device · [ANTAG] Bennett thread · [GFX] motion-graphic beat

**Tempo law:** One continuous acceleration from S2 through S5's apex; S6 breaks the rhythm (constriction, not deceleration); S7 changes key entirely; S8 returns to S1's pulse. The audience should *feel* the assembly line's tempo entering and leaving the film.

**Throughput drumbeat:** The recurring metric (hours→minutes, engines/day, bombers/hour) appears exactly once per industrial section (S3, S4, S5, S7) as a [GFX] beat — same graphic language each time, scale rising.

**Act mapping (for beat placement):**

- Act II = S4–S5 (false belief escalated to apex; costs accruing)
- Act III = S6–S7 (double erasure: mythology, then geopolitics)
- Coda = S8 (frame closes; desire→need pivot lands)
- **Midpoint of film:** S5's napkin-sketch beat (5.3) — sketch-to-steel, finally his own sketch. ◆

## SECTION 1 — THE RETURN (Framing Device / Cold Open) — ~6%

**Does:** Poses the memory question through the 2026 family; withholds all machinery; installs the frame the film must close.

**Beats:**
- 1.1 [FRAME][EMO] Cold open: family in transit to Copenhagen; the question stated as inheritance — who was he, and does anyone know?
- 1.2 [FRAME] Object beat: the memoir *My Forty Years with Ford* in a descendant's hands — a book almost nobody read, written so somebody would.
- 1.3 [EMO][GEO] The name "Sorensen" spoken in Denmark; emigration records; the loop Denmark→America previewed as the film's geography.
- 1.4 [FRAME] ◆ First and only S1 metaphor touch: patternmaker defined in one breath — the man who makes the mold; the casting never carries his name. Question locked.

**Transition OUT → S2:** Match on hands — a descendant's hands on the memoir CUT TO young Sorensen's hands on wood. First hard cut of the film; present tense won't return until S8.
**Rhythm note:** Quietest pages of the treatment. No statistics, no factories, no Ford.

---

## SECTION 2 — THE PATTERNMAKER (1881–1907) — ~9%

**Does:** Origin compressed to essentials; plants wound + false belief; establishes patternmaking as master metaphor and Piquette as first cathedral.

**Beats:**
- 2.1 [EMO][GEO] Danish emigration in brief — what leaving costs, what arriving demands; skill as the immigrant's only passport.
- 2.2 [IND] ◆ Foundry apprenticeship: wood, sand, iron; the craft of translation defined — sketch becomes pattern becomes casting. [GFX] introduce the pattern/casting schematic language here, at its smallest scale.
- 2.3 [EMO] Wound + false belief stated plainly: be indispensable and you cannot be erased. (This sentence-idea recurs at 6.5 shattered, 8.3 resolved.)
- 2.4 [IND] Arrival at Ford; Piquette Avenue; first meeting of Ford's sketches and Sorensen's hands — the translation relationship begins.

**Transition OUT → S3:** The metaphor scales — a single pattern on a bench DISSOLVES TO the same schematic multiplied into flow [GFX]. Craft becomes system.
**Rhythm note:** Compress ruthlessly (Spec risk note). Slowest visual tempo; warm textures. Get to Ford fast.

---

## SECTION 3 — THE LINE (1908–1914) — ~14%

**Does:** Delivers the moving assembly line as invention *and* first theft of credit; establishes Ford–Sorensen partnership; installs the labor counterweight.

**Beats:**
- 3.1 [IND] Highland Park; the problem stated: the Model T's demand vs. static assembly.
- 3.2 [IND] Collaborative truth: Sorensen, Martin, Avery, Klann — the chassis-drag experiment; Sorensen's *drive* as differentiator, not sole authorship. (R2 mitigation lives here.)
- 3.3 [GFX][IND] ◆ Throughput drumbeat #1: 12.5 hours → 93 minutes, animated arithmetic; the geometry of flow as the century's new pattern.
- 3.4 [GEO] The $5 day; mass society born; the world notices "Fordism" — including admirers the legacy would disown (flagged, not dwelt on).
- 3.5 [EMO] First theft: press and myth assign it all to Ford. Sorensen's response is not protest but harder work — false belief *rewarded*, therefore reinforced. Act I closes on this.

**Transition OUT → S4:** Sound bridge — line rhythm rises into foundry roar; machine-gray palette ignites to iron-fire. Scale jump #1.
**Rhythm note:** Acceleration begins and never fully stops until 5.4. Every technical beat resolves into a human stake within the same beat (R1 hard rule).

---

## SECTION 4 — THE GAMBLE (1919–1932) — ~15%

**Does:** Peak-power Sorensen; the V-8 monoblock as impossible feat that earns the name "Cast Iron Charlie"; seeds Bennett and the moral cost; opens the global thread.

**Beats:**
- 4.1 [IND] The Rouge: the whole world of making in one plant; Sorensen as its operating will.
- 4.2 [IND][EMO] The gamble: one-pour monoblock, $50M in the Depression's teeth; failure means ruin — indispensability wagered to prove itself.
- 4.3 [GFX][IND] ◆ Throughput drumbeat #2: the casting problem solved; 3,000 engines/day; the patternmaker's craft at monumental scale — same schematic, bigger mold. Nickname earned literally.
- 4.4 [EMO][ANTAG] The cost ledger opens: speed-up, shop-floor fear Sorensen himself administers; Bennett's Service Department rising in the margins. Antagonist seeded (R3 requires this here).
- 4.5 [GEO] Global thread opens: Ford's world plants, Sorensen's Europe oversight; the pattern begins exporting itself (R4 mitigation starts here, not in S7).

**Transition OUT → S5:** Rivera's Detroit Industry murals as pivot image — industry mythologized — then radio: war. Iron-fire palette cools toward silver.
**Rhythm note:** Most operatic register; heroization peak deliberately counterweighted by 4.4 in the same section, never deferred.

---

## SECTION 5 — THE ARSENAL (1941–1943) — ~16%

**Does:** Apex of the through-line and the film's midpoint; Willow Run nearly fails, then triumphs; vindication arrives maximally hollow.

**Beats:**
- 5.1 [GEO][IND] The ask: bombers by the ton; mass production drafted into civilization's defense; Washington enters the story.
- 5.2 [IND] A mile-long plant willed into existence; aluminum, not iron; aircraft tolerances vs. automotive habits.
- 5.3 [IND][EMO] ◆ **MIDPOINT.** The napkin/hotel-room layout: sketch-to-steel one last time — and for once the sketch is *his*. The metaphor's hinge: he is finally the author.
- 5.4 [EMO][GEO] "Willit Run?" — the failure year; public doubt; labor migration, women workers, chaos. The struggle beat the 63-minute figure must be earned through (Spec S5 risk).
- 5.5 [GFX][IND] Throughput drumbeat #3: a B-24 every 63 minutes; the apex number lands.
- 5.6 [EMO] Hollowing: the triumph is credited to "Ford," to America — the bigger the win, the less it is his. Wound now structural. Act II closes.

**Transition OUT → S6:** Hard tonal break — aerial vastness CUTS TO a corridor interior; the factories leave the film. News of Edsel.
**Rhythm note:** Peak velocity through 5.3, strain through 5.4–5.5, air out of the room at 5.6.

---

## SECTION 6 — THE FALL (1943–1944) — ~15%

**Does:** Full third-act treatment of the Bennett conflict and the exit; shatters the false belief; faces Sorensen's own culpability.

**Beats:**
- 6.1 [EMO] Edsel dies at 49 — the system's moral cost made flesh; the fall's trigger. Sorensen's guilt (he served the regime that ground Edsel down) stated, not excused.
- 6.2 [ANTAG] Bennett realized in full: the terror apparatus, the senescent king who feeds it; production vs. fear as rival theories of the company (R3 thesis stated on screen).
- 6.3 [GEO] Washington's alarm: a war-critical company in chaos; succession as quasi-state matter.
- 6.4 [EMO][ANTAG] The regency maneuver: Sorensen's push for Henry Ford II — his last act of translation, this time of power rather than steel.
- 6.5 [EMO] ◆ The discard: forty years, ended in a phone call. False belief shatters — indispensable never meant safe. The machine runs on without him: his success, proving his disposability.
- 6.6 [ANTAG] Bennett's mirror-fall (HFII fires him in a sentence) — fear evaporates the moment the king does; only the method was real.

**Transition OUT → S7:** Noir constriction OPENS abruptly to Caribbean light and horizon — the film changes key. The most violent tonal cut in the film, by design.
**Rhythm note:** No factories on screen; faces, corridors, documents. Rhythm constricts rather than slows.

---

## SECTION 7 — EMPIRE OF SAND (1944–1968) — ~15%

**Does:** Second life and second erasure; self-authored legacy built (Willys, CESOR, the estates) then taken by geopolitics; earns the ending.

**Beats:**
- 7.1 [IND] Willys-Overland: the Jeep turned civilian — proof he was the method, not the company. [GFX] Throughput drumbeat #4, smallest and last: the pattern still works in his hands alone.
- 7.2 [EMO][IND] ◆ CESOR Farm: his own name on the enterprise at last; hands on the machine in personal demonstration — the patternbench beat (2.2) rhymed at life's other end. Desire (credit) apparently fulfilled.
- 7.3 [GEO] The private empire: yacht *Helene*, Cuban estate, Virgin Islands — read as case study of postwar Caribbean capital strategy, not eccentricity (Spec integration rule).
- 7.4 [GEO][EMO] Expropriation: the Revolution takes the Cuban holdings — history repeats the theft, mythology's work now done by geopolitics. The wound's final test.
- 7.5 [EMO] The memoir written: the last casting he pours is his own story — self-mythology as the final act of a man terrified of erasure. (Book plants the S8/S1 object rhyme.)

**Beat law for this section:** Never epilogue. Each beat escalates the erasure stakes; 7.4 is the emotional keystone of the whole film per Spec §5.
**Transition OUT → S8:** The memoir closes CUT TO its obituary-thin public reception, 1968 — then the same book in the family's hands (rhyme with 1.2). Present tense returns.
**Rhythm note:** Release then hollowing; Caribbean color drains beat by beat from 7.3 → 7.4.

---

## SECTION 8 — REMEMBRANCE (1968 / 2026) — ~10%

**Does:** Closes the frame; converts desire (credit) to need (pattern as legacy); answers the opening question honestly — partially, not fully.

**Beats:**
- 8.1 [EMO] 1968: death and near-silence; the brevity of the obituaries as evidence of the forgetting.
- 8.2 [GEO][IND] Legacy audit: Fordism's afterlife — reconstruction, Toyota, globalization; every assembly line on earth an unsigned casting.
- 8.3 [FRAME][EMO] ◆ The pivot lands: what survives is pattern, not signature. The false belief's true answer, spoken over the family in Copenhagen — the wound not healed, but understood.
- 8.4 [FRAME] The family as custodians: the return itself is the remembrance he never received; seekers → witnesses → custodians completed.
- 8.5 [GFX][FRAME] ◆ Final graphic callback: the pattern/casting schematic from 2.2, closed — mold lifted, casting standing alone. The film's own act of remembering made lightly explicit. End.

**Transition OUT:** None — frame closed against 1.1.
**Rhythm note:** S1's pulse, now charged. Forgetting stays partly unhealed (Spec S8 risk); no swelling resolution.

1. **Metaphor touchpoints (◆):** 1.4, 2.2, 3.3, 4.3, 5.3, 6.5, 7.2, 8.3, 8.5 — one per section minimum, midpoint and ending anchored. Codex may not add competing metaphors.
2. **False-belief arc:** stated 2.3 → rewarded 3.5 → wagered 4.2 → hollowed 5.6 → shattered 6.5 → tested 7.4 → resolved 8.3. This chain is load-bearing; beats may be expanded but not decoupled from it.
3. **Bennett thread [ANTAG]:** seeded 4.4 → escalated (implicit, wartime margins) S5 → full 6.2/6.4 → mirror-fall 6.6. No Bennett before S4; no Bennett as footnote in S6.
4. **Throughput drumbeat [GFX]:** exactly four instances — 3.3, 4.3, 5.5, 7.1 — identical graphic grammar, rising scale, then deliberately diminished at 7.1.
5. **Hands motif:** 1.4/2.2 (patternbench) → 5.3 (napkin) → 7.2 (tractor) → 8.4 (memoir in family hands). Human constant per Spec §4.
6. **Frame budget:** [FRAME] beats confined to S1 and S8 (plus the 7.5→8 object rhyme). No present-day intrusions in S2–S7.
7. **Ford screen-time law:** Ford appears only inside Sorensen beats; he anchors no beat of his own (R6).
8. **Labor counterweight:** 3.4, 4.4, 5.4 carry the human-cost ledger; expansion may not soften them (R2, Spec §5).

# SPEC AMENDMENT — §2/S3-A1

## Title

Section 3.4 Geopolitical Framing and Verification Semantics Amendment

## Summary

Aligns the locked treatment specification with `TREATMENT_DRAFT_v1.1` domestic-only framing in beat 3.4. Preserves beat order, tags, labor counterweight placement, and the global-thread constraint.

## Amendment Details

- **Amendment ID:** Spec §2/S3-A1
- **Affected specification:** `TREATMENT_FRAMEWORK_SPEC_v1.md`, Section 2 — Section 3; related geopolitical-layer constraints in Sections 5 and 8.
- **Affected treatment:** `TREATMENT_DRAFT_v1.1`
- **Reason:** The v1.1 correction removes foreign-regime admiration from beat 3.4 and retains geopolitical globalization for the designated global-thread beats.
- **Scope:** Semantic specification only. No beat, tag, transition, rhythm note, or section-order change.

## Updated Clause

Section 3.4 remains the labor-counterweight beat. Its Fordism reception may address domestic wage, consumption, company-control, and social effects only. Global/export analysis remains restricted to beats 4.5, 7.3, 7.4, and 8.2. Beat 3.4 must not introduce foreign-regime admiration or foreign doctrinal adoption.

For Section 3.4, `[VERIFY]` flags now mean: verify domestic contemporary evidence supporting the beat's wage, social, company-control, or domestic Fordism-reception claims. They no longer require foreign-press or foreign-doctrine citations for that beat. Existing `[VERIFY]` flag presence remains mandatory.

All other geopolitical constraints, structural rules, beat tags, and labor-counterweight rules remain unchanged.
